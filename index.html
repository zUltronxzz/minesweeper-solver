Algorithm & Intelligence Breakdown

1
Gaussian Elimination (RREF)
Reduces boundary clue equations into linear combinations to solve complex overlapping subsets in microseconds.
2
Connected Component CSP
Partitions independent frontiers and backtracks valid mine configurations to find 100% safe tiles and flags.
3
50/50 & Probability Halting
Calculates exact combinatorial probabilities. When no moves are 100% safe, it pauses and highlights the safest tile.
